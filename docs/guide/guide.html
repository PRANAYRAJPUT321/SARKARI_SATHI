Sarkari Sathi – User Guide

Your daily companion to a Sarkari Naukri · Built by Pranay

This application is made by Pranay for government exam aspirants in India. Sarkari Sathi brings everything you need for Banking (SBI, IBPS, RRB), RBI, SSC, Railways (RRB), Insurance (LIC, NIACL) and Intelligence/Police (IB, SSC GD/CPO) exams into one free app: full mock tests with the real marking scheme, previous-year pattern papers, cut-offs, a syllabus tracker, a study planner with official exam dates, phone notifications and an AI help assistant. Open it at sarkari-sathi-blue.vercel.app

1
Open & install the app
Tap Install app in the top bar. Android: confirm Install (or Chrome ⋮ → Install app). iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Laptop: install icon in Chrome/Edge address bar.
2
Create your account
Sign up free with your name, email, password, category (for cut-off comparison), target exams (first = primary) and daily study goal in hours.
3
Your Dashboard
Greets you by name; shows the daily goal ring, streak & XP, exam countdowns, readiness %, the coach's next steps, today's plan and upcoming official exam dates.
4
Take a mock test
Mock Tests → choose exam → Full mock, Fresh random mock, PYP-pattern paper (2016–2025) or Sectional test. Real timing, sections and negative marking.
5
Use the test screen
Palette: green answered, red not answered, grey not visited, purple marked. Keys 1–5, N, P, M. Sections lock in sectional exams. Answers autosave.
6
Read your report
Score vs cut-off, seconds on every question vs ideal time, time sinks, rushed guesses, marks lost to negative marking, topic accuracy, solutions & bookmarks.
7
Practise every day
Practice → Daily Challenge (12 Qs, keeps the streak) and 15-question topic tests. Weak topics (<60%) are flagged automatically.
8
Track the syllabus
Syllabus Tracker: set each of 56 topics to Learning / Revising / Mastered. Open a topic for notes, formulas, shortcut tricks and solved examples.
9
Plan with the calendar
Study Planner: add timed tasks or Auto-generate a day-by-day plan till your exam (topics, mocks/day, Sunday revision). Official exam dates are pre-marked.
10
Turn on notifications
Notifications or Profile → Turn on. Get a morning plan (~6–7 AM IST) and a reminder when each task starts. iPhone: install the app first.
11
Ask Sathi (AI help)
Tap Ask Sathi (bottom-right) on any page for help using the app – install, notifications, reports, planner, exam dates.
12
More tools
Exam Explorer, Cut-offs, Analytics, GK Flashcards, Tips & Tricks, Focus timer, dark/light theme (moon icon) and Profile for exams, dates & goal.

Tips for best results

• One full mock a day; analyse it for as long as you took it.
• Skip a question after ~1.5× the ideal time; return later.
• Guess only when you can eliminate two options.
• Keep the Syllabus Tracker updated so your plan and readiness stay accurate.
• Exam dates appear only when IBPS / SSC / RRB announce them; cut-offs come from official results – always confirm on the official website.
